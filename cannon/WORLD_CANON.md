# Union of American States: World Canon

This directory is an internal development reference and is not shipped with the mod. It records the facts, motives, and voices that new gameplay and narrative content must follow.

## Source priority

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. Direct project direction recorded here.
2. `DESCRIPTION.txt`, which preserves the public premise and the USA and American-state perspectives.
3. Existing UAS events, news, focuses, decisions, and country histories that do not conflict with this reference.
4. Vanilla Hearts of Iron IV content only when UAS has deliberately retained it.

Vanilla history is not presumed to have happened. A vanilla event, leader, modifier, decision, or diplomatic assumption is an implementation artifact until it is reconciled with this world.

## Foundational timeline

- The Great Depression breaks the United States instead of merely weakening it.
- On October 24, 1929, the South issues a second Declaration of Independence.
- California declares independence on October 25.
- The federal government folds rather than fighting an immediate second civil war. Its leaders evacuate the mainland, Hawaii, and Alaska and establish a government in exile in Britain.
- President Hoover orders Michigan to become independent shortly before the evacuation. Michigan does not join the first secession and spends the following years trying to keep a fragile interstate peace.
- Britain decides that a collapsed America is a world-scale danger. The British Crown and government fund, arm, transport, and shelter the exiled federal government while keeping direct British intervention politically deniable. British troops wearing American uniforms secure the federal return foothold.
- Texas and Alabama initially cooperate. Their alliance breaks after Bibb Graves takes power in Texas through an election widely regarded as rigged.
- The federal government returns to Washington and calls its first operations policing actions against domestic occupation, not foreign conquest.

## The central conflict

The American continent is the principal theater of the coming world war. Every major American claimant offers a different answer to the same question: what replaces the failed United States?

The four American majors are always:

- UMI — Michigan
- UAL — Alabama
- UTX — Texas
- UCA — California

The exiled and returning USA is a federal claimant, but it is not one of the four American majors when mechanics explicitly refer to that group.

## Narrative rules

- UAS is dramatic, pulpy alternate history with a satirical edge. It should be specific rather than generic.
- Name the leader, capital, faction, courthouse, rail line, border road, radio station, ledger, party committee, or military room involved.
- Do not write a vanilla event and merely substitute an American tag. Start from the UAS political situation.
- A reference to Congress must describe the mod's federal institution at that moment. Vanilla US Congressional mechanics and assumptions are not canon.
- External powers must act for the motives above, not because vanilla history expects a particular treaty or war.
- Historical AI is the canonical route. Alternate AI rules may depart from it deliberately, but their departure must be legible.

## Art rule

Final content should not ship with an anonymous generic leader portrait or generic news image. A temporary placeholder is permitted only when it is recorded in the asset backlog for replacement.
